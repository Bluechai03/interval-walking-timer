## Voice and Tone Framework

The site speaks in first person, plainly and warmly. It states what was built and decided, names its evidence, and does not oversell. Exclamation marks stay at the two places that invite contact. Words that exist to sound confident ("really", "deliberately") are cut.

## Terminology

| Term | What it means to the reader | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Crew | A group of friends who walk the same plan on the same clock | Room, club, group |
| Walk with friends | The feature's name; a crew is what you start inside it | Walk together, rooms |
| Activity | Walk, Elliptical or Bike; changes the words only | Workout type |
| Case study | The page at `/work/interval-walking-timer` | Write-up |

## Copy Deck

### Home page

| Where | Current | Proposed | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Projects intro | "Each preview is the real thing." | "Each preview is a small working sample of the project." | The Strut preview is a two-phase sample of the timer, not the app, so "the real thing" overstates it. A reader who opens the app sees the difference. |
| Strut card | "I'm now adding shared crews, so friends can walk in step." | "It also works for the elliptical and bike, installs on your Home Screen, and friends can start a crew and walk in step. Written in TypeScript, with a Supabase backend and over 200 tests." | Crews are built and tested, so "now adding" is stale. "Crews" is not explained on the card. The backend and test count are the evidence a hiring manager looks for. |
| Hero link line | "Read the Strut case study · Live" | "Read the Strut case study · The app is live" | "Live" alone does not say what is live. |
| About, first paragraph | "If a site doesn't look good and feel easy on a phone, I'm not impressed by it, so that's the bar I hold my own work to." | "Whatever I build has to look good and feel easy on a phone, and that is the bar I hold my own work to." | Leads with what the reader gets, and drops a judgment of other people's sites. The hero and the About paragraph also repeat the same claim; this version keeps it once in the body and the hero keeps its own line. |
| Experience, "Early proofs of concept" | "Often handed a new idea before it has a plan." | "I'm often handed a new idea before it has a plan." | Fragment, and the other items are written in the first person. |
| Experience, "Live interfaces" | Title: "Live interfaces" | Title: "Real-time interfaces" | Names the technique a hiring manager searches for. |
| Contact | "I'd be really happy to chat!" | "I'd love to chat!" | One word shorter, same warmth. |
