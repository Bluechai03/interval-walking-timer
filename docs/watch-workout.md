# Start an Apple Watch workout from Strut

iPhone with an Apple Watch only. The timer cannot start a workout by itself, so it runs a Shortcut you make once.

1. Open **Shortcuts** on your iPhone and tap **+**.
2. Add the **Start Workout** action. Set Type to **Walking**, Location to **Outdoor** (make a second one for **Indoor**), Goal to **Open**.
3. Name it, for example **Strut outdoor**, and tap **Done**.
4. In Strut, scroll to **Apple Watch workout** and type the exact name.

When you tap **Start**, Strut starts the timer, flashes the Shortcuts app for a moment, and returns. Your Watch starts the workout straight away.

Nothing can end the workout from Shortcuts. End it on your Watch, or from the bar at the top of your phone.

If the Shortcut name is empty or wrong, the timer still starts as normal.
