# Strut

A timer for Japanese interval walking: alternating fast and slow walking so the walker gets the benefit of hard effort with recovery in between.

## Structure of a walk

**Interval walking**:
The training method as a whole: repeated rounds of fast and slow walking, classically 3 minutes each for 5 rounds.
_Avoid_: Interval (for a single phase), IWT

**Plan**:
The configured sequence of phases the walker intends to do: how long each fast and slow phase lasts and how many rounds.
_Avoid_: Program, routine, workout

**Phase**:
One continuous stretch of walking at a single pace, either fast or slow.
_Avoid_: Mode, interval, segment, set

**Round**:
One fast phase followed by one slow phase.
_Avoid_: Set, rep, cycle

**Walk**:
One run through a plan, from Start until the last phase ends.
_Avoid_: Session, workout, exercise

**Activity**:
What the walker is doing: walking, the elliptical, a bike or a run. It only changes the words they read and hear, such as "Walk fast" or "Push harder". The plan, the timer and the rooms work the same. Walking unless the walker picks something else, and in a room the host picks it for everyone.
_Avoid_: Exercise type, mode, workout type

## Announcements

**Cue**:
Anything the app does to tell the walker something without them looking at the screen: a beep, a vibration, or spoken words.
_Avoid_: Alert, notification, alarm

**Phase cue**:
The cue at the start of a phase, or at the end of the walk. It says the pace and the phase's target heart rate.
_Avoid_: Transition cue

**Countdown cue**:
A spoken cue partway through a phase that says how many seconds remain and what comes next. Each phase has one at 15 seconds left and one at 5 seconds left, skipped if the phase is not longer than that.
_Avoid_: Warning, reminder

**Quiet mode**:
A setting that turns off countdown cues while keeping phase cues, beeps and vibration. Off by default, remembered between walks, and can be changed during a walk. Not the same as Mute, which silences every cue's sound.
_Avoid_: Silent mode

**Mute**:
A switch that silences the beep and the spoken words of every cue, while vibration still plays on phones that have it. Off by default, remembered between walks, and can be switched during a walk. Not the same as Quiet mode, which only drops the countdown cues.
_Avoid_: Silent mode, sound off

## Rates

**Heart rate**:
The walker's heartbeats per minute, written in bpm. Each phase has a target heart-rate range.
_Avoid_: Pulse, bpm (on its own)

**Target heart rate**:
The single heart rate the walker aims for in a phase: the middle of that phase's target range.
_Avoid_: Goal bpm, goal heart rate

**Cadence**:
The walker's steps per minute, written as steps/min.
_Avoid_: bpm, pace, step rate

**Tempo**:
A song's beats per minute, used when matching music to a phase.
_Avoid_: bpm (on its own), speed

## Walking with friends

**Room**:
A shared walk that several people take at the same time. One host creates it, everyone follows the host's plan on the same clock, and it closes a few hours after the walk ends. Holds up to 8 people.
_Avoid_: Lobby, group, party, session

**Host**:
The member who creates a room, sets its plan, starts the walk and can remove people. Anyone can host.
_Avoid_: Owner, leader, admin

**Member**:
Anyone in a room, including the host.
_Avoid_: Player, participant, user

**Guest**:
Everyone who uses a room: a person with a name and no account, whose name and walks stay on their phone. Later, a guest may be able to keep their walks by signing in.
_Avoid_: Anonymous user, visitor

**Join code**:
The four letters shown on the host's screen that let someone join a room, along with a link. It stops working when the room closes.
_Avoid_: Room ID, PIN, invite code

**Nickname**:
The name a guest picks, 2 to 16 characters and unique within the room.
_Avoid_: Username, handle

## Starting a walk

**Get ready**:
The countdown, 5 seconds by default, between tapping Start and the first phase. Can be set to 0 for none.
_Avoid_: Pre-roll, warm-up
